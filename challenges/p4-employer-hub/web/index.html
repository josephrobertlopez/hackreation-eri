Match your real work to skills a candidate shared

Tell us your industry and three to five jobs that keep coming up. Then open a profile a candidate chose to share. The hub shows where the two line up, and says exactly which line of yours and which skill of theirs produced each suggestion.

Nothing you type leaves this page. There is no account, no server, no storage and no time limit. Close the tab and it is gone.

What this will not do

  • It will never ask you, or the candidate, about a disability or a diagnosis. There is no such field in this tool.
  • It does not score, rate or rank anybody, and it will not put two people in an order.
  • It does not decide anything: not hiring, not a change to how work is done.
  • It does not answer legal questions. It points you at JAN, the EEOC and Wisconsin DVR, who can.
