Show what you can do

Make a short page about your skills. You choose everything that goes on it.

  • There is no timer. You can stop and come back to any part.
  • There is no score. Nothing here rates you or ranks you.
  • We never ask about health, disability or a diagnosis.
  • Nothing leaves this page. There is no sign-up and no upload.

Start here

You can fill this in yourself, or start from a finished example and change it.

1. About you

Use any name you like. You do not have to use your legal name.

A first name, a nickname, or just initials is fine.

You never have to share health or disability information here. It is your page and your choice. You can leave that out and talk about the work instead.

2. Your skills

Pick three or more. Tick the ones you can do, then press the add button.

Or write a skill of your own

Write what you do, like “Water the plants”.

4. Save or share your page

Nothing is ticked to start with. Tick what you want to include, then save it as a file. The file is yours: you decide who ever sees it.
