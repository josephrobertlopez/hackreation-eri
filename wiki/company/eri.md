# Employment Resources, Inc. (ERI)

**One line:** a Madison, Wisconsin nonprofit (founded 1990) that helps people with disabilities get and keep jobs **without losing the benefits and health care they depend on.**

- **Mission:** "practice and promote innovative services to advance employment opportunities" for people with disabilities.
- **Vision:** people with disabilities can pursue financial security and meaningful work without fear of losing health care or other essential benefits.
- **Values:** Quality, Accountability, Integrity, Inclusiveness, Respect, Trust.
- **HQ:** 4604 Cottage Grove Road, Madison, WI 53716 · (608) 246-3444 · [eri-wi.org](https://eri-wi.org)
- **Footprint:** statewide in Wisconsin, with staff in Madison, Milwaukee, Green Bay, and northeast and northwest WI. Ticket to Work clients come from WI, OH, IN, IL, MN, IA and MI. ERI also runs national technical-assistance web projects.
- **Size:** about 29 staff ([staff page](https://eri-wi.org/staff/)); 10-person board ([board](https://eri-wi.org/about/board/)).

## What ERI does
| Line of work | Plain-English version | Paid by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Work Incentives Benefits Counseling (WIBS) | "If I take this job, what happens to my SSI/SSDI/Medicaid?" ERI does intake, gets signed releases, verifies benefits and writes a personal benefits report | DVR, IRIS, care managers refer |
| WIPA | Same help, under ERI's federal SSA contract. ERI is Wisconsin's **sole** WIPA provider | SSA cooperative agreement |
| Ticket to Work (ERI PLUS) | Job search and support for SSI/SSDI recipients working toward leaving cash benefits; mostly virtual | SSA Ticket payments |
| Employment counseling and Supported Employment | Find the right job, then help keep it (job coaching, retention) | **Wisconsin DVR** fee-for-service |
| Youth transition (VFPS, CLTS) | Planning for life after high school | Counties, DHS |
| Comprehensive Community Services (CCS) | Mental health and substance-use recovery support | Dane County |
| Pre-driving assessment | Is driver training realistic? | Various |
| Business and employer services | Recruiting and retention consulting for employers hiring people with disabilities | Fee-for-service |
| Training and design | Builds websites, e-learning and LMS content for DHS, universities and national TA centers | Contracts and grants |

## History highlights ([about](https://eri-wi.org/about/))
- 1990: founded
- 1992-95: trained Wisconsin's first work-incentives benefits specialists
- 2000: ERI's benefits-counseling model became an SSA "best practice," feeding the federal program now called WIPA
- 2008: started the Ticket to Work Employment Network (ERI PLUS) and e-learning for benefits specialists
- 2014: Wisconsin PROMISE federal demonstration site
- 2018: built the Explore Work curriculum and site
- 2021: became the sole Wisconsin SSA WIPA provider
- 2024 (Nov): Kweku-TeAngelo Cargile Jr. named Executive Director ([Madison365](https://madison365.com/employment-resources-inc-names-kweku-teangelo-cargile-jr-as-new-executive-director/))
