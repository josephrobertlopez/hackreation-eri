## Judging

Judges: the ERI liaison and one or two developer judges. Joey hosts and does not score. **Rubric version: draft as of Thu Oct 29; Joey changes "draft" to "final" after the Fri Oct 30, 6 pm confirmation with the ERI liaison.** Any change after that is posted in the Meetup comments and here. Each judge scores only the rows they own, 0 to 5 per row (0 = nothing, 3 = works with gaps, 5 = we would use it). When two judges own a row, the row score is their average, to one decimal. Presentation is the mean of all judges' scores, to one decimal. A team's weighted total is the sum over the rows of (row score / 5) x weight, so the most it can be is 100. Worked example: ERI liaison scores 4 on Solves (24 points), 4 on Adopt (12 points); the developer judge 3 on Accessible (15 points), 4 on Works (16 points); Presentation mean 4.0 (8 points). Total 24 + 15 + 16 + 12 + 8 = 75.0. Tie order: higher "Solves ERI's problem", then higher "Works end to end", then Joey flips a coin. Joey prints a judge sheet with this table and score boxes. Judges never get a team password. If no developer judge comes, the ERI liaison scores their two rows too and also applies the README cap. If the liaison is absent, Joey names a stand-in before 2:30: another ERI staff member or a developer judge, never Joey. If GitHub is not reachable at the venue, the judge sees the README line in the team's demo on the venue laptop; Joey has it open.
