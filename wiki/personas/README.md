## The numbers behind them
Source: [Wisconsin Rehabilitation Council 2025 annual report (DVR, Oct 2024 to Sep 2025)](https://www.dwd.wi.gov/dvr/partners/wrc/pdf/2025-wrc-annual-report.pdf)

| Stat | Value |
| --- | --- |
| People DVR served | 33,687 |
| Share under age 25 | about 40% |
| Top primary disabilities | Mental illness 19.7%, autism 12.5%, intellectual 9.9%, ADHD 9.8%, orthopedic 9.2% |
| Successful job outcomes (90+ days) | 3,429 |
| Average wage at closure | $17.04/h (median $15.00) |
| Average hours | 24.9 h/week |
| Top job types | Transportation and material moving; food prep and serving; sales; office and admin; building and grounds |

Wisconsin had 189,194 working-age people with disabilities employed in 2023, a record ([DWD](https://dwd.wisconsin.gov/press/2024/241023-record-high-disability-employment.htm)).
