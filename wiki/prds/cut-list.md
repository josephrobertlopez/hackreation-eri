# Cut list (Occam's razor)

> **RETIRED history for Hackreation 2026. Not in scope.** It describes the old three ideas (01 to 03), not ERI's four projects. See [docs/eri-problem-statements.md](../../docs/eri-problem-statements.md).

The razor: **asked for by ERI?** · **usable by ERI's web team within two volunteer evenings?** · **demo-able in about 4 hours?** · **safe without real data?**

| Idea | Verdict | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Full job board / matching platform | Cut | Needs a real job feed, accounts and maintenance. Four teams would build four job boards nobody adopts. The core value moved into PRD 02 |
| Standalone benefits estimator | Merged into 02 | Same user and moment; one tool beats two |
| Form-filling guide (DVR, SSA, benefits forms) | Cut for now | Forms vary and change; wrong guidance has real consequences; it's content-heavy, not code; it overlaps ERI's Benefits Application Services. Good *post-event* content project with ERI's benefits team |
| Accessible intake + release forms | Cut | Touches PII and consent; needs ERI's IT and legal; can't ship from a hackathon |
| Report / case-note drafting with AI | Cut | DVR requires consumer consent for AI; real notes are sensitive. Revisit after ERI has an AI policy |
| Turning-18 navigator | Cut | ERI already has Before Age 18 and its planning tool; better to improve that site than build a new one |
| Spreadsheet pivot "skill" for billing | Kept inside 01 | Allowed as a team's approach in 01 (synthetic data only at the hackathon) |
| Multi-funder revenue platform | Kept as the *story* of 01 | Built for DVR + mocks only; the pattern is what ERI keeps |
