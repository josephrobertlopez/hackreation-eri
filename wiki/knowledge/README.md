## Headline findings (as of 2026-10-23)

- **DVR waitlist:** an Order of Selection waitlist began December 2025 (peak 7,610 on 2026-06-02). DVR began serving from it on 2026-06-02 and aims to clear it by 2027-06-30. A separate provider waitlist (2026-08-31) had 160 of 199 people statewide in WDA 10, ERI's home area: a capacity opportunity.
- **DVR provider agreement:** any AI tool used with a consumer's information requires that consumer's prior written or verbal consent.
- **2027 SSA numbers are out** (3.6% COLA): SGA $1,770, TWP $1,270, SSI FBR $1,029. The Wisconsin SSI supplement rose 10% to $92.16 on 2026-05-01.
- **Section 503's 7% goal and self-ID form were rescinded** (final rule 2026-08-21, effective 2026-09-21). **WOTC lapsed** for hires after 2025-12-31 unless Congress reauthorizes it.
- **14(c) subminimum wage continues:** DOL withdrew its phase-out rule on 2025-07-07.
- **ADA Title II web deadlines moved** to 2027-04-26 (large) and 2028-04-26 (small); the standard is WCAG 2.1 AA.
- **Medicaid work requirement (2027)** applies to BadgerCare childless adults; SSI-Medicaid, MAPP, Family Care/IRIS and SSA-disabled people are generally not subject or are exempt.
- **42 CFR Part 2** compliance date was 2026-02-16.
- **ERI finances:** revenue has been flat at about $3.0-3.3M since FY2018, with net assets covering about 2.8 months of expenses. FY2025 program revenue fell 10.7%.
