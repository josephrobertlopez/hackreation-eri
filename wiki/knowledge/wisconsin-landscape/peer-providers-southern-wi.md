# Peer disability employment providers in southern Wisconsin

## TL;DR
- Southern Wisconsin has a **mixed field**:
  - large multi-service nonprofits (Goodwill SE WI, LSS, Easterseals WI)
  - legacy rehabilitation and day-service agencies that are moving toward community employment (Curative Care Network, Opportunities Inc., Threshold, Kenosha Achievement Center)
  - disability-specific organizations (DSAW)
  - Centers for Independent Living (IndependenceFirst, Access to Independence)
  - case management and IRIS agencies (Avenues to Community, Progressive Community Services, LSS Connections)
- **Project SEARCH** shows who holds DVR youth contracts in each region: **Goodwill** (Milwaukee and Racine), **CEO** (Milwaukee and Waukesha), **MARC** (Madison and the Dells), **Threshold** (West Bend), **DSAW** (Watertown) and **Community Solution** (Janesville).
- **Capacity is short where ERI is.** On 2026-08-31, **WDA 10 had 160 of the 199** DVR consumers statewide who were waiting for a provider. That makes WDA 10 providers more likely partners than competitors for overflow.
- This page is **low confidence**. Many homepages did not show DVR vendor status or service counties. Read every **(verify)**.

## How it works
- DVR does not have one public "vendor list" that this pass could open. The most reliable public signals of who holds which DVR roles are the **Project SEARCH roster** and provider-waitlist bulletins. Use DVR's provider directory or a WDA office to confirm vendor status.
- Many legacy agencies combine **facility-based or day services** paid by Family Care with **community employment** paid by DVR. Employment First (Act 178) and DHS's CIE priority push those agencies toward integrated jobs.
- Overlap with ERI is heaviest in **WDA 10** (Dane, Jefferson): Opportunities Inc., DSAW, MARC, Access to Independence and Progressive Community Services. In Milwaukee, Goodwill, CEO, Curative and IndependenceFirst are the anchors.

## Caveats and gotchas
- **"Curative Connections"** is a different organization from Milwaukee's **Curative Care Network**. Curative Connections is believed to be based in the Green Bay area, outside the southern region. **(verify)** which one the brief meant.
- **Easterseals.** The Middleton-based Easterseals Wisconsin site did not mention DVR or Family Care. A separate **Easterseals Southeast Wisconsin** has served Milwaukee in the past, but its page could not be opened. **(verify)** that it exists and what it offers.
- **"Ability Center"** and **"Badger Bay"** from the brief: **no southern Wisconsin disability employment provider by either name was confirmed.** **(verify)** or drop them.
- **Avenues to Community** exists, but it is a CLTS case management agency in Dane County, not an employment provider.
- Other likely peers that were **not checked** in this pass: Kandu Industries (Janesville; site blocked automated reading), Careers Industries (Racine), Lakeside/other Waukesha agencies, Work Opportunities in Rural Communities, the Center for Independent Living for Western WI (out of region) and IPS county programs. **(verify)** before adding them.

## How ERI / a hackathon tool should use this
- Use this page for **referral routing** (for example, a Kenosha family goes to KAC, or Watertown goes to DSAW Project SEARCH) and for **partnership mapping**, not for competitive claims.
- A tool **must not** rank providers or imply DVR endorsement. Consumers have **informed choice** of provider under their IPE. Show options neutrally and send people to their DVR counselor.
- Re-verify the list every six months. Provider rosters change with each DVR contract cycle, and the current one runs 2026-07-01 to 2028-06-30.
