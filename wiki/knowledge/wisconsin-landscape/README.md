# Wisconsin disability employment landscape (index)

## TL;DR
- Four state agencies run the system:
  - **DWD**: DVR, the workforce boards, apprenticeship and the Equal Rights Division
  - **DHS**: ADRCs, Family Care, IRIS, CLTS and CCS
  - **DPI**: school transition
  - **WisDOT**: s. 85.21 transportation aid
- Counties and nonprofits deliver most services.
- Southern Wisconsin spans **five WDAs**: 1 (Kenosha, Racine, Walworth), 2 (Milwaukee), 3 (Waukesha, Ozaukee, Washington), 10 (South Central, including Dane) and 11 (Southwest, including Rock).
- The facts that matter most in late 2026:
  - the **DVR waitlist** (December 2025 to a target of 2027-06-30)
  - the **WDA 10 provider-capacity gap**
  - the **2027 MCO changes in the southeast counties**
