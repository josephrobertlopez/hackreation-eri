# Wisconsin long-term care system and ongoing employment supports

## TL;DR
- **ADRCs** (Aging and Disability Resource Centers) are the front door to adult long-term care. They offer free options counseling, disability benefit specialists (ages 18-59) and Family Care/IRIS enrollment. Each of the 10 southern counties has its own ADRC. The statewide finder line is **844-947-2372 (844-WIS-ADRC)**.
- **Family Care** (managed care through MCOs) and **IRIS** (self-direction through an IRIS Consultant Agency) are Medicaid waivers. They pay for **supported employment, small-group supported employment, prevocational services and vocational futures planning (VFPS)**.
- In Supported Employment, **DVR pays for up to 24 months of ongoing support** and then hands off to a long-term support (LTS) funder. For adults 25 and older the IPE must name that funder. Family Care, IRIS and CLTS are treated as **comparable benefits**, and the case should move to them after **90 days of job stability**.
- MCO choice is changing. In mid-2026, Milwaukee and Dane each had four Family Care MCOs. DHS has announced new MCOs for **Kenosha, Racine, Walworth, Waukesha, Ozaukee and Washington starting in 2027**, subject to protests and CMS approval.

## How it works
- **ADRC first.** An adult who may need long-term care goes to the ADRC. The ADRC screens functional eligibility, gives options counseling and enrolls the person in Family Care, Partnership, PACE or IRIS. ADRC **disability benefit specialists** help people ages 18-59 with public benefits.
- **Family Care.** An MCO gets a monthly payment for each member. The member's interdisciplinary team authorizes services in the member-centered plan. Members can choose among the MCOs certified in their county.
- **Family Care Partnership** adds acute and primary health care to Family Care.
- **IRIS.** The participant directs their own budget. The ICA consultant helps write the plan, and a fiscal employer agent handles payroll.
- **CLTS (children).** This is a Medicaid waiver for children with disabilities, run through **county health and human services agencies**. DHS says the **waitlist has ended**: under "continuous enrollment," children are enrolled as soon as they are found eligible.
- **HCBS employment services** (DHS Employment Initiatives):
  - *Individual supported employment* covers discovery, job development, job coaching, workplace personal assistance, Partners with Business, and work incentive benefits counseling.
  - *Small-group supported employment* serves groups of 2-6 workers.
  - *Prevocational services* are community-based skill building.
  - *VFPS* is team-based planning toward competitive integrated employment (CIE).
  - Wisconsin's Employment First law (2017 Act 178) names CIE the preferred outcome.
- **The handoff from DVR.** The IPE names the LTS source. The team reviews the plan at the Career Profile, at the 60-day on-the-job meeting and before the transition. Transition happens only after 90 days on the job. DVR closes the case 90 days after the transition. The provider earns the $1,300 transition fee only if transition happens within six months of systematic instruction. DVR may pay LTS for youth under 25 for up to 48 months.

## Caveats and gotchas
- **Anthem in Milwaukee** appears in the 06/2026 key-contacts PDF, but the What's New page did not describe a Milwaukee award. **(verify)** whether Anthem is enrolling Family Care members in Milwaukee now.
- **The 2027 southeast changes are not final.** Anthem is new in Kenosha, Racine, Walworth, Waukesha, Ozaukee and Washington. Inclusa is new in Kenosha and Racine. iCare, My Choice and UnitedHealthcare get Partnership expansions. Award letters went out on 2026-07-14, and contracts wait on any protests. Partnership changes also need CMS approval. **(verify)** in January 2027.
- **iCare** is in the key-contacts PDF without a county list. It was added to Partnership in Dane and Rock in January 2025 (DHS release). **(verify)** current Partnership counties.
- DHS has ordered **My Choice Wisconsin** to take corrective action in both programs. No dates were shown.
- The DHS Employment Initiatives page does not say whether someone must try DVR before an MCO will fund supported employment. The HCBS Employment and Daytime Service Manual (P-03793) may say. **(verify)**
- The CLTS page did not give an age limit or the date continuous enrollment started. **(verify)**

## How ERI / a hackathon tool should use this
- Build the LTS funder into intake. Collect "Family Care / IRIS / CLTS / CCS / none" and the MCO or ICA name early, because DVR needs it in the IPE.
- A tool can show a person's county ADRC and the MCO options for that county. It **must not** recommend one MCO over another or determine program eligibility. Send people to the ADRC for that.
- Flag cases at 60 days on the job so the team can confirm the transition plan before the 90-day mark, which protects the $1,300 transition fee.
- Re-check the MCO table after 2027-01-01.
