# Employer and business networks for disability hiring (southern WI)

## TL;DR
- **DVR Business Services Consultants (BSCs)** are DVR's employer-facing staff, organized **by WDA**. There are 12 WDA positions plus a section chief, and Milwaukee has a North and a South BSC. In the 2026 listing, the **Milwaukee South and WOW (WDA 3) positions were vacant**. DVR's services to businesses are free and voluntary (800-442-3477).
- DVR's main employer incentive is **On-the-Job Training (OJT)**: **50% of wages for up to 90 days**, or **100% of wages for up to 500 hours in 12 months for students**. "Other Services" include Partners with Business, Skills to Pay the Bills and **Walgreens REDI**.
- **MMAC**, founded in 1861, represents about **2,000 member businesses employing more than 300,000 people**. Its affiliate, the **Milwaukee 7**, is the regional economic development partnership for Kenosha, Milwaukee, Ozaukee, Racine, Walworth, Washington and Waukesha counties. Neither one listed a disability-inclusion program.
- A **Wisconsin Disability:IN or Business Leadership Network affiliate could not be confirmed** from an opened page. **(verify)**

## How it works
- **Dual customer.** The VR system treats employers as customers as well as job seekers. A BSC builds a "customized service plan" with a business. Then DVR counselors and contracted providers like ERI bring candidates, job coaching and retention support.
- **Who does what.** DVR BSCs and workforce boards handle the employer relationship at the regional level. **Providers do the placement work**: job development, job and task analysis, systematic instruction and retention, under DVR's statewide services. Partners with Business is the service where a provider trains **co-workers as natural supports**.
- **Recent DVR employer initiatives** in the FFY2025 report include **autism training with Walmart** and sessions with **SSM Health and Sodexo**.
- **Accommodation basics** employers ask about: under Wisconsin law, the employee generally has to ask. The employer must accommodate unless it would cause a hardship. The employer can choose any accommodation that works.

## Caveats and gotchas
- **Disability:IN Wisconsin / Wisconsin BLN.** The national affiliate list and wibln.org could not be read in this pass. **(verify)** whether a Wisconsin affiliate is active and who runs it, before naming one.
- **Chambers.** Local chambers (Madison Region Economic Partnership, Greater Madison Chamber, Waukesha County Business Alliance, Racine/Kenosha chambers) were **not opened**. **(verify)** each before listing contacts.
- Federal **WOTC** lapsed for hires after 2025-12-31 unless Congress reauthorizes it, and the Section 503 7% goal was rescinded effective 2026-09-21. Don't pitch employers on either. See [hiring incentives](../employment-law/hiring-incentives-wotc-schedule-a.md) and [Rehabilitation Act](../employment-law/rehabilitation-act.md).
- BSC vacancies change often. Check the BSC contact page before sending an employer to a WDA.
- Do not promise employers OJT reimbursement. DVR approves it case by case, and it needs an active IPE, which the waitlist affects.

## How ERI / a hackathon tool should use this
- **Where ERI Business Services fits:**
  1. A **single point of contact** that offers employers a bundle: candidates, job and task analysis, job coaching, Partners with Business co-worker training and benefits-aware onboarding.
  2. **Coordinate with the WDA 10 BSC** and WDBSCW so employers are not approached by several agencies with no coordination.
  3. Use DVR's **provider waitlist gap in WDA 10** (160 consumers on 2026-08-31) as a capacity story for employers who need talent.
  4. **Training and design services**: accessibility and accommodation workshops for MMAC, chamber or WDB members.
- A tool could log **employer needs** (roles, shifts, location against transit, accommodations already in place) and match them to candidate profiles. It **must not** disclose a candidate's disability or benefits to an employer without the candidate's written consent.
