# Disability advocacy and legal help in Wisconsin

## TL;DR
- **Disability Rights Wisconsin (DRW)** is the state's protection and advocacy organization. Since **2021-09-30** it has also run the **Client Assistance Program (CAP)** for people with concerns about DVR or an Independent Living Center. DATCP ran CAP before that. DRW's toll-free number is **800-928-8778**.
- **Employment discrimination** complaints under the Wisconsin Fair Employment Act (Wis. Stat. §§ 111.31-111.395) go to DWD's **Equal Rights Division** within **300 days**.
- **BPDD** (the DD Council) and the **Council on Physical Disabilities** (Wis. Stat. 46.29) advise on policy and do not provide services. **Survival Coalition** is the cross-disability policy coalition of more than 20 statewide organizations.
- **Legal Action of Wisconsin** merged with Judicare on **2026-01-01**. It gives free civil legal help with benefits, criminal records and driver's licenses (both employment barriers). Intake is **855-947-2529**.
- **The Wisconsin ABLE law** (SB 668, signed **2024-04-04**) told DFI to create an ABLE program. As of 2026 DFI says a multi-state partnership is "the most viable option," and **no Wisconsin plan has launched yet**.

## How it works
- **DVR disagreements.** The person first asks the counselor or supervisor. If that does not resolve it, the person can call CAP. Most CAP help is information, referral or brief coaching. CAP **cannot represent someone at a DVR impartial hearing that has already been requested**, and its capacity for new cases is "very limited." DRW asks people to read its self-advocacy resources first.
- **Family Care / IRIS disputes.** DRW runs the Family Care and IRIS Ombudsman Program for members under 60. **(verify)** the age split: the ombudsman for members 60 and older is usually the Board on Aging and Long Term Care.
- **Workplace discrimination.** State law protects people with a current impairment, a record of one, or a perceived one. It covers alcoholism and drug addiction, and AIDS. Employers must provide reasonable accommodation unless it would cause a hardship. The employee usually has to ask for it. State law **does not** cover discrimination by association, but the ADA does (EEOC Milwaukee office).
- **Policy work.** BPDD funds projects and runs leadership training such as Partners in Policymaking, a six-session class in Madison and online. Survival Coalition issues joint positions on state budgets. In 2025 it backed the enacted budget's Medicaid and special-education increases.

## Caveats and gotchas
- CAP has been **at DRW since 2021-09-30**. Older materials still name DATCP.
- DRW's homepage did not list office locations. **(verify)** the Madison and Milwaukee office addresses before printing them.
- **"ABLE Law."** This page reads it as Wisconsin's ABLE accounts law (SB 668, 2024). If the hackathon brief meant a legal aid organization called ABLE, that is a different item, and none was found for Wisconsin. Ohio's "ABLE" (Advocates for Basic Legal Equality) is not a Wisconsin provider. **(verify)** what was meant.
- **ABLE accounts are still open to Wisconsin residents** through other states' plans. DFI gives the disability-onset rule as "before age 46." This is believed to be the federal ABLE Age Adjustment Act change effective 2026-01-01. **(verify)** For state tax treatment DFI points to DOR.
- The **ERD's own phone** was not on the disability page. Only the main DWD line was shown. **(verify)** the ERD direct line.
- The **Survival Coalition website** was not confirmed from an opened page. **(verify)**
- Applications for the BPDD Partners in Policymaking class were due 2026-10-23, today. The page shows a 2026-2027 class schedule.

## How ERI / a hackathon tool should use this
- Put a **"Get help with a dispute"** route in any consumer tool. DVR problems go to CAP at 800-928-8778. Workplace discrimination goes to ERD, with a 300-day clock. Family Care or IRIS problems go to the DRW ombudsman. Records or license barriers go to Legal Action at 855-947-2529.
- A tool **must not** tell someone whether they have a valid claim, or calculate deadlines for their specific facts. It can show the general 300-day rule and send them on.
- Do not collect details about a dispute in a hackathon prototype.
