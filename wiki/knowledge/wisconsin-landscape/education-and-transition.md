# School-to-work transition in Wisconsin

## TL;DR
- **Wisconsin starts transition planning at 14, earlier than the federal rule of 16.** A Postsecondary Transition Plan (PTP) is required in the first IEP in effect when the student turns 14, and it is updated every year (Wis. Stat. 115.787(2)(g)1). It needs measurable goals for **education/training, employment and, where appropriate, independent living**.
- **DVR Pre-ETS** serves students **ages 14-21** with an IEP, a 504 plan or a documented disability. Students can get it without a full DVR application. DVR must spend **15% of its budget** on Pre-ETS. In FFY2025, **3,852 students** were served and $16.18M was spent.
- **Project SEARCH** has **29 sites statewide**. About 12 are in southern Wisconsin, mostly hosted by hospitals, with providers such as Goodwill, CEO, MARC, Threshold, DSAW and Community Solution.
- Inclusive college options include **Edgewood University's Cutting Edge** (Madison, active in 2026), **UW-Whitewater LIFE** (ages 18-25, residential) and **Bethesda College at Concordia University Wisconsin** (Mequon; verify current status).

## How it works
- **Schools (DPI).** The IEP team writes the PTP from age-appropriate transition assessments. The district must provide at least one transition service each year. It must invite the student, and with consent it must invite any outside agency (DVR, county, MCO) that may provide or pay for services. Community work placements alongside non-disabled peers count as time in the regular classroom.
- **Transition Improvement Grant (TIG).** This DPI-funded project (witig.org) has about 10 staff. It aims to improve PTP quality, graduation and post-school outcomes, and it supports **County Communities on Transition (CCOT)**.
- **Interagency Agreement on Transition.** DPI, DVR and DHS share responsibility. The **Transition Action Guide (TAG)** is the technical assistance guide that goes with the agreement.
- **Pre-ETS through DVR.** Services can come from DVR staff, contracted providers such as Student Work-Based Learning vendors, or Project SEARCH skills training. **"Potentially eligible" students** can get Pre-ETS without an IPE. Transportation, systematic instruction and personal assistance need an IPE.
- **Under the waitlist (December 2025 onward).** Students who already had documented Pre-ETS can continue. Adults on the waitlist cannot attend Project SEARCH with DVR funding.
- **Let's Get to Work** was a Waisman Center (UCEDD) project with pilot high schools from **spring 2012, for about four years**. **73%** of the students it served had one or more paid work experiences, which is a strong predictor of adult employment.
- **Employment First (2017 Wisconsin Act 178)** requires DWD, DPI and DHS to set joint CIE targets every two years and report each year.

## Caveats and gotchas
- The 14-year start is **state law**. Federal Indicator 13 measures from 16. Do not apply the federal age to Wisconsin students.
- **Bethesda College** status comes from a 2019 WUWM article. **(verify)** that it still runs at Concordia Mequon.
- **UW-Whitewater LIFE.** The page did not show cost or Comprehensive Transition Program (CTP) federal aid status. **(verify)**
- Edgewood is now **"Edgewood University"**. Cutting Edge applications run August 1 to November 30. Its final-year off-campus jobs are arranged with DVR.
- Who partners with BPDD on Let's Get to Work, and which districts took part, were not on the opened page. **(verify)**
- The Project SEARCH list has "TBD" cells. Check before naming providers in public.
- Some sources say "Milwaukee Zoo" and some "Milwaukee County Zoo". The DVR list says "Milwaukee Zoo".

## How ERI / a hackathon tool should use this
- ERI runs **VFPS youth transition navigation** and **CLTS youth and family services**. A tool can produce an age-based checklist: 14 means a PTP and a DVR Pre-ETS referral; about 2 years before exit, apply to DVR; 17.5 means guardianship or supported decision-making planning; before 22, line up the LTS funder.
- A tool **must not** write IEP goals or tell a family what a school must provide. Send disputes to DPI or DRW.
- Use the Project SEARCH table for referrals by county. The Kenosha, Walworth and Ozaukee gap is a possible partnership opportunity.
