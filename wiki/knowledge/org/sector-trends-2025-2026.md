# Sector trends 2025-2026

## TL;DR
- **Wisconsin DVR is the biggest near-term shock.** DVR started a waitlist for new participants in **December 2025** after a $4.6M state shortfall. It reached **7,610 people on 2026-06-02**. Recovery money came from the Joint Finance Committee ($0.6M SFY26 + $6.4M SFY27, 2026-06-02) and an **$11.05M federal reallotment** (2026-08-31). The goal is to clear the waitlist by **2027-06-30**. DWD warns funding will not cover the next biennium.
- **H.R. 1 (P.L. 119-21, enacted 2025-07-04)** adds an 80-hour/month Medicaid "community engagement" rule. It covers Wisconsin's roughly 190,000 BadgerCare childless adults (April 2026 enrollment; DHS's 2026-07-30 webinar says about 200,000 are subject, see [Medicaid work requirements](../benefits/medicaid-work-requirements-2027.md)) and takes effect in 2027. It also widened SNAP ABAWD time limits to ages 18-64.
- **SSA** set a staffing target of 50,000, down from about 57,000, and is cutting from 10 regions to 4 (2025-02-28). The WIPA 2021 agreements ran to **2026-06-30**. What follows is not confirmed **(verify)**.
- **14(c):** DOL withdrew the phase-out rule on 2025-07-07, so subminimum-wage certificates continue. **ODEP:** FY2026 enacted **$43.0M** (flat), with FTE down from 65 to 46. The FY2027 request is $33.81M.

## How it works
- **DVR funding** is 78.7% federal RSA and 21.3% state. When the state match falls short, DVR can't draw all its federal money, so it uses an Order of Selection: Category 1 (most significant), then 2, then 3. Vendors like ERI get fewer new purchase orders, while existing plans continue.
- **BadgerCare work requirement:** Wisconsin did not adopt full ACA expansion. It does cover childless adults up to the poverty line, and the federal rule applies to that group. Medically frail people and others are exempt. DHS must send a noncompliance notice and allow 30 days before ending coverage (per the CMS IFR summary).
- **WIPA** is a fixed set of cooperative agreements. A gap between agreements, or a smaller award, hits benefits-counseling capacity directly.

## Caveats and gotchas
- **SSI/SSDI recipients:** the sources opened for this page did not settle this. The [Medicaid work requirements](../benefits/medicaid-work-requirements-2027.md) page, built from the Wisconsin DHS work page and 2026-07-30 webinar, lists SSI Medicaid, MAPP, Family Care, IRIS, people with an SSA disability determination and Medicare enrollees as not subject. Use that page.
- No public record of the post-2026-06-30 WIPA awards, extension, or Wisconsin amount was found **(verify)**. ERI's WIPA page is still live.
- **PABSS** (Protection and Advocacy for Beneficiaries of Social Security) funding status for 2025-2026 was not confirmed **(verify)**.
- **SSA field office** closures and visit-reduction plans were reported in the press (December 2025). The primary SSA release we opened makes no field-office statement **(verify)**.
- **VR State Grants FY2026 final appropriation** was not confirmed. Only the request was seen **(verify)**. The Supported Employment State Grants zero-out is a request **(verify)** whether it was enacted.
- Grants.gov was not searched in this pass (search budget ran out).

## How ERI / a hackathon tool should use this
- **Benefits counselors** will get questions about the 80-hour BadgerCare rule. A KB entry should explain it, link to DHS, and route people to WIPA or Disability Rights Wisconsin. It must NOT tell anyone whether they are exempt.
- **ERI planning:** model FY2026 DVR fee revenue as down, with recovery in 2027. Build a waitlist re-activation surge plan (Category 1 first).
- A tool must date every rule it shows. These rules are moving, and stale guidance can cost people coverage.
