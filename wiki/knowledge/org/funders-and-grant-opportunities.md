# Funders and grant opportunities for ERI

## TL;DR
- **Best near-term fits:** United Way of Dane County (Otto Bremer "disability services" fund, up to $75K; workforce training up to $47K; Impact Grants reopen spring 2027), Madison Community Foundation (Dane County program or capacity grants; LOI due in June), and **Kessler Foundation** (national; ERI's ~$3.07M budget clears the $2M Signature and $1M Community thresholds).
- **Milwaukee expansion funders:** Greater Milwaukee Foundation (workforce development is a priority) and United Way GMWC. GMWC's "Reducing Barriers to Employment" cycle runs 07/2026-06/2028, is closed, and **no further cycles are anticipated**.
- **Weak fits as checked:** Bader Philanthropies lists no workforce or disability area today. DOL ODEP lists no grants and its budget is shrinking. The RSA Disability Innovation Fund goes to state VR agencies, so ERI could only join as a subrecipient.
- **Tech in-kind:** Microsoft 365 Business Basic free up to 300 users, Azure $2,000/yr, Google Ad Grants up to $10,000/month, Salesforce 10 free licenses.

## How it works
- Most local funders (UW Dane, MCF, GMF) fund **projects or capacity, not operations**. A good pitch pairs a concrete project (e.g., a benefits-counselor knowledge base) with measurable outcomes.
- Kessler requires collaborators and other funding. A pitch with WI DVR or an employer partner plus a hackathon-built prototype is a stronger story.
- Federal disability-employment money mostly reaches ERI **through** state agencies (DVR, DHS) or SSA. That is why diversified private money matters.

## Caveats and gotchas
- Grants.gov was not searched in this pass (search budget ran out) **(verify)** for open SSA, ODEP or RSA notices.
- Kessler's 2009 Signature page said the focus was New Jersey. The current FAQ says grants are limited to the U.S. and territories, and Special Initiative grants are NJ-only. Confirm current Signature geography **(verify)**.
- Bader historically funded workforce programs (older news headlines). Its current site does not list them **(verify)** before writing it off.
- UW Dane's FEMA EFSP window was listed both as "closed" and with September 2026 dates on the same page.
- Dollar amounts and deadlines change each cycle. Re-check before applying.

## How ERI / a hackathon tool should use this
- A grants calendar tool could track the windows above (UW Dane spring, MCF March-June, Bader May 31/Nov 30, Kessler annual) with an owner and status for each.
- An AI grant-drafting helper must use only ERI-approved facts (from the [financial history](eri-financial-history.md) and [programs](eri-programs-deep-dive.md) pages) and must flag claims that need sources. It must NOT invent outcome numbers.
