# ERI programs deep dive

## TL;DR
- ERI runs **three different payment engines**: (1) **outcome/milestone fees** (DVR, Ticket to Work), paid only after a result is documented; (2) a **cooperative agreement** with SSA for WIPA, a budgeted award (2021 RFA: $50K-$300K per award, period 2021-07-01 to 2026-06-30); (3) **contracts** with Dane County (CCS), Wisconsin DHS, and federal TA centers for training and web work.
- WIPA: ERI says it has been the **sole Wisconsin WIPA agency since 2021**. SSA funds 74 WIPA agencies nationally. ERI's dollar award could **not** be confirmed (usaspending.gov did not render) **(verify)**.
- Public outcome claims are thin: "more than 80+" Ticket "graduates" (no timeframe), an "Excellent" EN satisfaction rating, and "hundreds" helped into jobs.
- 29 staff are listed. Most are Employment Specialists (6) and benefits staff (6), plus a 6-person training/web team.

### Ticket to Work payments, 2026 (Milestone/Outcome system)
| Payment | Trigger | SSDI | SSI |
|---|---|---|---|
| Phase 1 milestones (4) | Earnings at or above $1,210/month for 1, 3 of 6, 6 of 12, 9 of 18 months | $1,904 each, $7,616 total | same |
| Phase 2 milestones | Earnings at or above SGA while still in cash status | 11 x $571 = $6,281 | 18 x $319 = $5,742 |
| Outcome payments | Benefits stop because of work | 36 x $571 = $20,556 | 60 x $319 = $19,140 |

## How it works
- **WIPA** is free benefits counseling by Community Work Incentive Coordinators (CWICs) for SSDI/SSI beneficiaries aged 14 to full retirement age who are working, have a job offer, are interviewing, are veterans, or are aged 14-25. SSA set it up under the Ticket to Work and Work Incentives Improvement Act of 1999. It is authorized under Section 1149 of the Social Security Act. Awardees must serve at least 5 business days a week and need SSA Tier 2 suitability for staff.
- **Ticket to Work** pays ERI only when an assigned beneficiary earns. A long-run SSDI outcome is worth up to $34,453 ($7,616 + $6,281 + $20,556) over several years. That is unrestricted revenue, but it lags service by months to years.
- **DVR** issues a purchase order per milestone. ERI earns nothing until the report is submitted and accepted. A returned report must be fixed within 10 business days. Preferred outcomes pay $400-$600 more per hire. Preferred-outcome criteria (DVR appendix): $2,200 for 35+ h/week and (health insurance or ≥ $16.00/h); $2,000 for 20+ h/week and (health insurance or ≥ $12.00/h). Source: https://dwd.wisconsin.gov/dvr/service-providers/tech-specs/appendix.htm
- **CCS** is a Dane County program for people with mental health or substance use needs. ERI provides the employment, benefits and education pieces. ERI's page says capacity is below demand.

## Caveats and gotchas
- The WIPA 2021-2026 project period ended **2026-06-30**. ERI's WIPA page was live on 2026-10-23, but we found no public record of the follow-on award, extension, or amount **(verify)**.
- ERI's dollar award on usaspending.gov could not be read (the site needs JavaScript) **(verify)**. The $300K ceiling is per award. ERI may hold more than one award **(verify)**.
- "More than 80+ graduates" is ERI's own wording. Its definition (Ticket outcome status? job?) and timeframe are not stated **(verify)**.
- ERI's employment-counseling waitlist is "closed to new referrals" (CCS page). That limits fee growth.
- DVR ran a statewide waitlist from December 2025. That cut new DVR purchase orders to all vendors until it began unwinding on 2026-06-02 (see [sector trends](sector-trends-2025-2026.md)).

## How ERI / a hackathon tool should use this
- Build for the **payment trigger**. A DVR report helper that hits the 5-business-day rule, or a Ticket earnings tracker that spots Phase 1 milestones, turns directly into cash.
- A benefits-counseling assistant must route to a CWIC (1-877-826-1752) and must NOT give individual benefits determinations.
- Do NOT store beneficiary PII in hackathon prototypes. WIPA staff need SSA Tier 2 suitability, and tools must respect that.
