# ERI consulting brief

## TL;DR
- ERI is a **$3.07M, 29-person specialist** organization. Its core assets are statewide benefits-counseling expertise (ERI describes itself as the sole Wisconsin WIPA agency since 2021; the 2021 project period ended 2026-06-30 and the follow-on award is **(verify)**) and a rare in-house web/LMS team. It runs on **thin margins**: 0.9% in FY2025, five loss years out of fifteen, and about 2.8 months of net assets.
- Revenue has been **flat at $3.0-3.3M for eight years** and depends on government rules it doesn't control (SSA, DVR, Dane County). The December 2025 DVR waitlist shows how fast fee income can stall.
- **Top 3 moves (highest impact per unit of effort):** (1) DVR billing integrity and days-to-report, (2) shift job development toward **preferred outcomes**, (3) a reviewed **knowledge base / AI assistant for benefits counselors** that stretches WIPA and CCS capacity.

## Strategic opportunities (ranked by impact / effort)

| # | Opportunity | Impact | Effort | Why (cited) | First step |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **DVR billing integrity**: report-deadline tracker, PO-to-payment reconciliation, returned-report fixes | High | Low | Fees pay only after reports land within 5 business days; returned reports must be fixed in 10 business days | Pull 12 months of POs vs paid invoices and measure leakage |
| 2 | **Preferred-outcome job development** | High | Low-Med | +$400-600 per SE hire; +$600 per JD retention; Rapid Hire $1,900 vs $1,600 | Get the DVR appendix criteria; aim job development at jobs that genuinely offer more hours, higher wages or insurance. The rate follows the job actually obtained and documented, never how a note is worded |
| 3 | **Benefits-counselor KB / AI assistant with human review** | High | Med | 6 benefits staff cover the whole state for WIPA; CCS "delays because demand is higher than our available capacity"; BadgerCare rule starts 2027 | Hackathon prototype on public SSA/DHS sources; CWIC sign-off on every answer |
| 4 | **Ticket payment capture** (earnings evidence, milestone claims) | Med-High | Low | Phase 1 milestones $1,904 each at $1,210/month earnings; SSA pays only on proof | Check every assigned Ticket against payment history |
| 5 | **Fix and monetize the web/LMS shop** | Med | Low (fix) to Med (sell) | VRTAC-QE ended; Training Center shows "$XX"; 6-person team already in place | Clean up the storefront in 30 days; one-page offer for TA centers and state agencies |
| 6 | **Productized employer services** (accommodation consulting via JAN/EARN, inclusive job posts, retention support; federal contractors as an outreach list) | Med | Med | Business page offers recruiting, retention and consultations. Note: Section 503's 7% goal was rescinded 2026-09-21 and WOTC lapsed for hires after 2025-12-31, so don't sell on those | Pilot 3 paid employer packages built on retention and accommodation value |

## Risks
- **Revenue concentration and policy risk:** SSA (WIPA and Ticket) and DVR together likely make up most revenue **(verify)** with Schedule B or the audit.
- **WIPA re-competition or gap:** a smaller or delayed award would hit benefits capacity directly **(verify)**.
- **DVR 2027-2029 biennium:** DWD says current funding won't sustain services. A second waitlist is possible.
- **AI risk:** a wrong benefits answer can cause an overpayment or lost coverage. Every answer needs human review, citations and dated rules.
- **Key-person risk:** a small leadership team, and a 6-person web team on a premium-plugin WordPress stack.

## KPIs to track (monthly unless noted)

| KPI | Definition | Why |
|---|---|---|
| DVR outcome rate | Closed-employed / DVR referrals accepted (rolling 12 months) | Core quality and fee driver |
| Preferred-outcome % | Preferred hires / all hires (SE and JD) | Fee premium capture |
| Days-to-report | Median business days from milestone event to DVR report submitted; % within 5 | Payment speed; leakage |
| Report return rate | Reports returned / submitted | Billing integrity |
| Revenue per employment specialist | Fee revenue / FTE specialists (quarterly) | Productivity |
| Ticket payments | $ and count of P1/P2/outcome payments; active Tickets assigned | Unrestricted revenue |
| WIPA reach | Beneficiaries served, BS&As completed, wait time to first contact | Contract performance |
| Months of net assets / cash | Net assets (and cash) / monthly expenses (quarterly) | Resilience |
| Web/LMS contract revenue and pipeline | $ booked, proposals out | Replacing VRTAC-QE |

## How ERI / a hackathon tool should use this
- Hackathon projects should map to opportunity #1, #3, #4 or #5 and report a KPI from the table.
- Tools must NOT make individual benefits determinations, store client PII in prototypes, or show unsourced outcome claims.
