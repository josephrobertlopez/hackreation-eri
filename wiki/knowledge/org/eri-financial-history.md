# ERI financial history (FY2011-FY2025)

## TL;DR
- ERI's revenue doubled from about $1.5M (FY2011-FY2015) to about $3.0-3.3M (FY2018-FY2025), then stopped growing. FY2025: **$3,065,352 revenue, $3,038,876 expenses, $26,476 surplus (0.9% margin)**.
- Margins are thin. Over 15 years ERI netted a cumulative **$298,935**, and 5 of the 15 years were losses (worst: FY2018, -$326,102).
- The mix shifted from contribution-led (80% in FY2011) to fee-led (55-71% program service revenue since FY2017). On the 990, "contributions" includes government grants such as the SSA WIPA cooperative agreement, so 45% "contributions" is not 45% private philanthropy.
- FY2020 shows a one-time bump ($312,953 surplus) in the year ERI received a PPP loan (approved 2020-04-07, $350K-$1M range, 40 jobs).

## How it works
- **Program service revenue** is what ERI earns by delivering services: DVR milestone fees, Ticket to Work payments, Dane County CCS billing, and training or web-design contracts. It pays only when work is done and reported.
- **Contributions and grants** on Form 990 Part VIII line 1 mixes government grants and cooperative agreements (SSA WIPA, state DHS projects, federal TA subcontracts if structured as grants) with private gifts. ProPublica's extract does not split line 1e (government grants) from private gifts.
- **Two growth steps:** revenue jumped from $1.54M (FY2015) to $2.19M (FY2016) and $3.03M (FY2018). The ERI timeline credits that era with customized employment in seven Wisconsin workforce areas (2017). After FY2018, revenue has stayed between $2.97M and $3.35M for eight years.
- **Losses cluster in transition years:** FY2018 (-$326K) came at the end of the growth spurt. FY2022 (-$161K) was the year ERI moved offices (the About page says it moved to the Acewood location in 2022).
- **Liabilities tripled in FY2022** ($255K to $867K) while revenue was flat. That matches the 2022 office move and would fit an operating lease being recorded on the balance sheet. **(verify)** in the audited statements / Schedule D.
- **FY2025 mix shift:** program service revenue fell $202,272 (-10.7%) and contributions rose $159,671 (+13.2%). The filing alone does not show which programs drove it. **(verify)**

## Caveats and gotchas
- ProPublica figures are extracted from IRS e-files. The full-filing view did not render when we opened it, so Part III (program expenses), Part VIII line 1e and Schedule O were **not** read. **(verify)** against the PDF or ERI's audit.
- **PPP accounting:** ERI's About page lists "Paycheck Protection Program (2020)" among its supporters, and pppreport.org shows the loan. The exact amount, forgiveness, and whether it was booked as FY2020 or FY2021 grant income are not confirmed. The FY2020 surplus is consistent with forgiveness being recognized in 2020. **(verify)**
- Net assets are not cash. Some of the $701K may be fixed assets or restricted money. Liquidity is **(verify)**.
- The FY2024 executive compensation figure covers an ED transition (ProPublica notes two EDs that year). Do not read it as a raise.
- Wisconsin DVR started a waitlist in December 2025 and began activating people from it on 2026-06-02, with a goal of ending it by 2027-06-30 (see [sector trends](sector-trends-2025-2026.md)). FY2025 numbers do not yet show that effect. FY2026 likely will. **(verify)** when the FY2026 990 is filed.

## How ERI / a hackathon tool should use this
- Treat ERI as a **cash-constrained, fee-dependent** organization. A tool that adds staff time without lifting billable outcomes or cutting admin work is a net cost.
- Good targets: speeding up DVR report submission (fees are paid only after reports are accepted), Ticket payment tracking, and grant reporting.
- A dashboard built from this page must label "contributions" as "contributions **and government grants**". It must not present 45% as private donations.
- Do NOT publish internal financial projections or non-public numbers in the wiki. Use only filed 990 data.
