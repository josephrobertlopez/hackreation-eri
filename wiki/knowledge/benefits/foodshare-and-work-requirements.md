# FoodShare (SNAP) work requirements in Wisconsin after H.R. 1

## TL;DR
- H.R. 1 (Public Law 119-21, signed **2025-07-04**, section 10102) raised the able-bodied adults without dependents (ABAWD) time-limit age from 18-54 to **18-64**. It lowered the child exemption to a child **under 14** and removed the exemptions for homeless people, veterans, and former foster youth.
- Wisconsin applied the age and child changes from **2025-11-01**. The tribal exemption started **2025-10-01**. Discretionary exemptions for ages 55-59 ended **2025-12-01**, and remaining changes, including ages 60-64, took effect **2026-03-01** (DHS Ops Memo 26-04).
- Rule: ABAWDs must work, volunteer or join a work program **80 hours/month**. Otherwise they get only **3 months of FoodShare in 3 years** (current period: 2025-01-01 to 2027-12-31).
- **Disability exempts people.** Getting any disability benefit (SSI, SSDI, VA disability, workers' comp), or being physically or mentally unable to work, makes a person "unfit for work".

## How it works
**Two separate rule sets.**
1. *Basic work rules* (ages 16-59): accept a suitable job, do not quit without good cause, and so on. People working 30+ hours a week, or earning the same at federal minimum wage, are exempt.
2. *ABAWD work requirement / time limit* (ages 18-64): meet 80 hours a month or have an exemption. Otherwise, after 3 countable months, FoodShare stops for that person until they meet the requirement for 30 days or become exempt.

**Exemptions in Wisconsin (DHS list, 2026).** Pregnant; unable to work because of physical or mental health (including chronic homelessness and getting disability payments); living with a child 13 or younger; tribal member; caring for a child 5 or younger in another home, or for someone who cannot care for themselves; getting or applying for unemployment compensation; enrolled at least half-time in school or training; in W-2; in AODA treatment.

**How disability is shown.** DHS memo 26-04 says getting any temporary or permanent disability payment from a government or private source qualifies as "unfit for work". It also lists examples of conditions such as long-term illness, depression, trouble concentrating, and trouble walking or climbing stairs. Self-attestation is generally accepted unless it is questionable.

**FSET (FoodShare Employment and Training).** Free and voluntary. It offers job search help, training (CNA, forklift, CNC and similar), GED/HSED, and support costs such as bus fare, tools and clothes, for up to 90 days after getting a job. Taking part counts toward the 80 hours. Household members 16+ can join even if they are exempt.

## Caveats and gotchas
- People 60-64 are **not** subject to basic work rules but **are** subject to the ABAWD time limit. FNS also bars mandatory E&T for ages 60-64.
- Homelessness alone is no longer an exemption. *Chronic* homelessness (no fixed nighttime residence, and none expected within 30 days) can still make someone "unfit for work".
- Parents whose only children are 14-17 are now subject to the time limit.
- A lost exemption mid-certification is not applied until the next interview screening (from 2025-11-01).
- The Wisconsin SSI supplement increase (2026-05-01) counts as income for FoodShare and may slightly reduce FoodShare (DHS Ops Memo 26-12).
- A separate DHS memo (Ops 26-31) covers time-limit suspensions for high-unemployment areas in federal fiscal year 2027. We did not open it. **(verify)** which Wisconsin areas, if any, are suspended.

## How ERI / a hackathon tool should use this
- A screener should ask about disability benefits first. Anyone on SSI or SSDI should see "People who get SSI or SSDI are often exempt from the FoodShare work requirement. Your income maintenance agency decides. Ask them, or call WIPA at 1-877-826-1752."
- For job seekers not on disability benefits, show the 80-hour options. Note that ERI or DVR employment services may count as a work program only if they are approved as one **(verify)** with the local FSET agency.
- When estimating income changes, remind users that higher wages can lower FoodShare. Route them to WIPA **1-877-826-1752** for combined benefits planning.
- Do NOT tell anyone they are exempt as a final decision. The income maintenance agency decides.
