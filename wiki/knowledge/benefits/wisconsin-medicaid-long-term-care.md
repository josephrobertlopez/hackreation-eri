# Wisconsin Medicaid and long-term care

## TL;DR
- **BadgerCare Plus** is income-based Medicaid for ages 0-64. Adults qualify at or under 100% FPL ($1,330/month for one person, from 2026-02-01). **SSI-related (EBD) Medicaid** is for people who are 65+, blind or disabled. It has a $2,000 asset limit and the $20 and $65-and-one-half income credits.
- Adult long-term care runs through **Family Care / Family Care Partnership** (managed care organizations) or **IRIS** (self-directed). In 2026, both use a **$2,000 asset limit** and a **$2,982/month** income limit. People with income up to $10,854.80 may qualify with a cost share.
- Children: **CLTS** waiver (under 22; the waitlist has ended) and **Katie Beckett Medicaid** (under 19; parents' income is not a barrier).
- All of these list supported employment and competitive integrated employment (CIE) as services. Members of Family Care, IRIS, SSI Medicaid and MAPP are not subject to the federal work requirement that starts 2027-01-01.

## How it works
**Which "door" a person uses.**
- *SSI recipient*: Wisconsin Medicaid comes with SSI (1634 state). It continues under 1619(b) while working.
- *Disabled, not on SSI, low income*: SSI-related (EBD) Medicaid, if assets are at or under $2,000. Otherwise BadgerCare Plus, if income is at or under 100% FPL.
- *Disabled and working with more income or assets*: the [Medicaid Purchase Plan (MAPP)](wisconsin-medicaid-purchase-plan-mapp.md).
- *Needs long-term care*: contact the local **Aging and Disability Resource Center (ADRC)**. The ADRC does the Long-Term Care Functional Screen and options counseling.

**Family Care.** A Medicaid long-term care benefit for adults 18+ who are frail elders or have a physical, intellectual or developmental disability. They must be Medicaid-eligible, functionally eligible, and have a long-term condition expected to last over 90 days. Members choose a managed care organization (MCO). The MCO plans and buys services. **Family Care Partnership** also covers acute and primary care.

**IRIS (Include, Respect, I Self-Direct).** The same people qualify, but the participant gets a budget and directs services. They hire their own workers through a fiscal employer agent and get guidance from an IRIS consultant agency. The listed employment supports include CIE exploration, individual and small-group supported employment, prevocational services, and vocational futures planning.

**Cost share.** In Family Care and IRIS, people with income above the $2,982 limit (up to $10,854.80) may owe a monthly cost share. It is based on income, housing and medical costs. More earnings can mean a higher cost share. That is a real "work cost" a benefits plan must model.

**Children.**
- **CLTS**: a Medicaid home and community-based waiver for children under 22 who need an institutional level of care. It funds non-medical supports. It now has continuous enrollment, with no waitlist.
- **Katie Beckett Medicaid**: Medicaid for children under 19 with a disability who need institutional-level care. Parents' income and assets do not block eligibility.
- At 18, the child's own income and resources start to matter (SSI parental deeming ends). This is the time to plan SSI, Medicaid and the move to adult long-term care.

**Employment services and Medicaid.** DHS policy calls CIE "the first and preferred employment outcome". In SFY 2021, only about 11% of working-age Family Care, Partnership and IRIS members had CIE. Their median pay was $9.50/hour for 12.5 hours/week. A person can get DVR services (where ERI is a Supported Employment vendor) and later long-term job supports through Family Care or IRIS. Long-term care programs usually expect DVR to be used first for services DVR can fund **(verify)** in the Family Care benefit package and IRIS policy manual.

## Caveats and gotchas
- People mix up BadgerCare Plus and EBD Medicaid. Disability-based coverage (EBD, MAPP, waiver programs) is the safer category for workers with disabilities. BadgerCare adults face the 2027 federal work requirement and the 100% FPL income cap.
- The $2,982 Family Care/IRIS limit equals 300% of the 2026 FBR (our arithmetic). It will change on 2027-01-01 with the new FBR ($1,029 x 3 = $3,087, unconfirmed). **(verify)** in DHS P-10059 2027 edition.
- The DHS pages we opened do not show the spousal impoverishment amounts or the cost-share formula. **(verify)** in the Medicaid Eligibility Handbook.
- Moving from 1619(b) to MAPP can keep Family Care or IRIS eligibility, but cost share and premium rules interact **(verify)** case by case with the ADRC or MCO.
- CLTS was renewed in 2022 for five years. Expect a renewal around 2027 **(verify)**.

## How ERI / a hackathon tool should use this
- A "which Medicaid am I on?" helper can map answers (SSI? long-term care? age? working?) to likely programs. It should then say "Confirm with ForwardHealth Member Services 800-362-3002 or your ADRC."
- Earnings estimators for Family Care or IRIS members must warn that cost share can change, and route the user to WIPA **1-877-826-1752**.
- For youth turning 18, show a checklist: SSI redetermination at 18, the end of parental deeming, CLTS ending at 22, and Katie Beckett ending at 19.
- Do NOT tell anyone they will or will not qualify for Family Care or IRIS. Functional and financial eligibility are decided by the ADRC and the income maintenance agency.
