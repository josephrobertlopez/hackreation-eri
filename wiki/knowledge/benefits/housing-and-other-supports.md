# Housing and other supports

## TL;DR
- **HUD Earned Income Disregard (EID) is closed to new families.** Under HOTMA, public housing agencies (PHAs) had to stop enrolling families after **2023-12-31**. HOTMA instead raised the elderly/disabled family deduction to **$525** and moved the medical expense threshold from 3% to **10%** of income, with phased hardship relief. HUD will enforce HOTMA sections 102/104 for all PHAs from **2027-01-01**.
- **The Wisconsin SSI state supplement rose 10% on 2026-05-01**, to **$92.16/month** (individual) and $145.26 (couple). It was the first increase since 1994. **SSI-E** (Exceptional Expense) adds a higher state amount: a $197.75 total state payment for an individual, so $1,191.75 combined with the 2026 FBR.
- **SSDI recipients can now get Wisconsin unemployment insurance (UI).** A federal court enjoined Wis. Stat. 108.04(12)(f) from **2025-07-20**. DWD is processing past claims for people denied between 2015-09-08 and 2025-07-29.

## How it works
**HUD EID (historical) and HOTMA.** EID used to exclude new earnings from rent calculations for some public housing and Housing Choice Voucher families with disabilities. Under the HOTMA income rule, no family can start EID after 2023-12-31. Families already in EID finish it under HUD guidance (PIH 2023-27, Attachment G). Since enrollment ended in 2023 and EID ran on a 24-month clock, few or none should still have it in late 2026 **(verify)** the end date with the PHA. Today the work-related help in HUD rent comes mainly from the $525 deduction, the medical/disability assistance expense deduction (over 10% of income), and **Family Self-Sufficiency (FSS)** escrow accounts. Rent is generally 30% of adjusted income, so higher wages raise rent.

**Wisconsin SSI supplement.** Wisconsin runs its own state supplementary payment (SSP). Anyone with a federal SSI payment of any amount gets the full state payment. Living-arrangement categories: independent living, or living in another's household. The state supplement is not COLA-adjusted. The 2026-05-01 increase came after 32 years with no change.

**SSI-E (Exceptional Expense).** For SSI recipients who either live in a qualifying substitute care setting with high costs, or live in a natural residential setting and need **40+ hours/month** of long-term support services. A county or long-term care agency certifies it.

**UI for SSDI recipients.** In *Bemke v. Pechacek* (W.D. Wis. No. 3:21-cv-00560), the court held on 2025-07-14 that Wisconsin's SSDI bar violated the ADA and the Rehabilitation Act by disparate impact. It enjoined enforcement from 2025-07-20. It then ordered remedies on 2025-08-20 for people denied UI or made to repay. The Legislature did not repeal the statute. DWD says it will process UI claims for SSDI recipients who meet all other UI rules. DWD is mailing notices to class members.

**How UI interacts with other benefits.** For SSI, UI is **unearned** income, so it reduces SSI almost dollar for dollar after $20. UI also counts as income for FoodShare and Medicaid. For SSDI, UI does not reduce the check, but statements made on a UI claim about being able to work could be looked at during a CDR **(verify)** with WIPA or Disability Rights Wisconsin.

## Caveats and gotchas
- The DWD SSDI program page could not be opened on 2026-10-23. We do not know if there was an appeal to the Seventh Circuit or whether the injunction was made permanent. The Clearinghouse listed the case as ongoing (summary updated 2026-02-19). **(verify)** the current status with DWD or the court docket.
- The SSI-E "$197.75" figure is the regular supplement plus the SSI-E add-on ($92.16 + $105.59). Do not add $197.75 on top of $92.16.
- The 1619(b) threshold chart for 2026 still uses the old $83.78 supplement (see the 1619 page).
- Wisconsin Homestead Credit and other state tax credits were not reviewed for this page. **(verify)** if a tool needs them.
- HOTMA rules apply to public housing and vouchers. Multifamily (project-based Section 8) timelines differ **(verify)**.

## How ERI / a hackathon tool should use this
- A benefits calculator for Wisconsin SSI must use the **current** state supplement ($92.16 from 2026-05-01) as a separate line. For SSI-E cases, use the total state payment.
- A rent estimator must not apply EID to new cases. It can show a "rent goes up about 30 cents per extra dollar of adjusted income" note, and route users to their PHA.
- Job-loss guidance for SSDI recipients can say "You may be able to apply for Wisconsin unemployment." It must add "Check with WIPA 1-877-826-1752 first, because UI affects SSI and FoodShare."
- Do NOT tell anyone they will get UI back pay. DWD decides.
