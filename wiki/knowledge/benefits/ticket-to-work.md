# Ticket to Work

## TL;DR
- Adults **18 through 64** who get SSDI or SSI based on disability can use their Ticket. It is free and voluntary. They assign it to one **Employment Network (EN)**, such as ERI PLUS, or use state VR (DVR).
- While the Ticket is assigned and the person makes **timely progress**, SSA does **not** start a medical Continuing Disability Review (CDR).
- ENs are paid only when the beneficiary earns. For 2026: Phase 1 milestones are **$1,904** each (4 milestones, at $1,210/month earnings). Maximum total payments are **$34,453** (SSDI) and **$32,498** (SSI) under milestone-outcome, or **$38,268 / $35,640** under outcome-only.
- Ticket Help Line: **1-866-968-7842** (TTY 1-866-833-2967), Monday to Friday, 8 a.m. to 8 p.m. ET.

## How it works
**Assigning a Ticket.** The beneficiary chooses an EN. They can talk to as many ENs as they like first. The EN checks eligibility with the Ticket Program Manager. The EN and the person sign an **Individual Work Plan (IWP)**. With state VR, the plan is an IPE. A person can work with only one EN at a time. Someone with an open DVR case finishes or closes it before getting EN services. DVR can hand off to an EN after closure (**Partnership Plus**) **(verify)**, since the pages we opened did not describe Partnership Plus.

**What ENs provide.** Career planning, job placement, and ongoing supports. ERI PLUS can pair this with WIPA benefits counseling, but WIPA is a separate, neutral service.

**How ENs are paid.** SSA pays ENs only on earnings evidence. The payment rate in effect in the month the milestone or outcome is reached applies.
- Phase 1 milestones: gross earnings of at least $1,210/month (2026 TWP level).
- Phase 2 milestones: earnings above SGA.
- Outcome payments: earnings above SGA **and** a $0 federal cash benefit.

**Timely Progress Review (TPR).** Every 12 months, SSA checks progress toward the work goal. Under 20 CFR 411.180:

| Ticket-use period | Work test (or education alternative) |
|---|---|
| 1st 12 months | 3 months with earnings at or above the TWP amount; or HS diploma/GED; or 60% of a degree or vocational program |
| 2nd (24 months) | 6 months at TWP level; or another 75% of program |
| 3rd (36 months) | 9 months above SGA; or finish a 2-year degree, certificate or vocational program |
| 4th (48 months) | 9 months above SGA; or more 4-year credits |
| 5th and later | 6 months with earnings that stop SSDI/SSI cash; or degree completion (through the 6th) |

Work and education percentages can be combined to total 100%.

**Medical CDR protection.** SSA will not start a medical CDR while the Ticket is "in use" and progress is timely. Protection does not cover work CDRs (reviews of earnings). Putting the Ticket in inactive status stops the protection for those months.

**Section 301.** If SSA finds medical improvement while someone is in an approved program that started before the improvement decision, benefits can continue. Approved programs include an EN's IWP, a VR IPE, a PASS, or an IEP for ages 18-21. SSA must find the program likely to lead to leaving the rolls.

## Caveats and gotchas
- The TPR table above is from the SSA-hosted CFR page. **(verify)** it matches the current eCFR text before using it in a tool.
- Ticket protects against **medical** reviews only. Earnings still reduce or stop checks under normal SSDI/SSI rules.
- SSA had not published 2027 EN payment rates as of 2026-10-23. Milestone thresholds will follow the 2027 TWP ($1,270) and SGA ($1,770 / $2,950) **(verify)** when SSA publishes "2027 Payments at a Glance".
- "Ticket to Work" ads are not always from SSA. Only official ENs listed on choosework.ssa.gov should be assigned Tickets.

## How ERI / a hackathon tool should use this
- An intake tool can explain Ticket in plain language. It can check eligibility questions (age 18-64, SSDI/SSI based on disability) and offer a warm handoff to ERI PLUS. It must also say the person can choose any EN or DVR.
- An EN revenue model can use the 2026 table above. It must mark rows as "payment rate when attained" and must never forecast an individual's earnings as fact.
- A TPR tracker for staff should show months worked at the TWP level versus SGA, by Ticket-use year.
- Always pair Ticket talks with benefits counseling: WIPA **1-877-826-1752**.
- Do NOT promise that Ticket "protects your benefits." It only stops medical CDRs while progress is timely.
