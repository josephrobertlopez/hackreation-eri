# SSDI work rules (TWP, SGA, EPE, Medicare while working)

## TL;DR
- In 2026, any month with gross earnings over **$1,210** uses one of 9 **Trial Work Period (TWP)** months, counted in a rolling 60-month window. Full SSDI is paid during the TWP.
- After the TWP comes a 36-month **Extended Period of Eligibility (EPE)**. SSDI is paid for months under **SGA**: $1,690/month (non-blind) or $2,830/month (blind) in 2026. It is suspended in months over SGA.
- The first SGA month after the TWP starts the cessation. SSDI is paid for that month and a 2-month **grace period**.
- Premium-free Medicare Part A continues for at least **93 months** after the TWP ends. **Expedited Reinstatement** is available for 5 years after benefits stop.

## How it works
**1. Trial Work Period.** Each month with gross earnings above $1,210 (2026) is a TWP month. Self-employed people also use one with more than 80 hours of work. After 9 TWP months in any 60-month window, the TWP ends. SSDI is paid in full for every TWP month, no matter how much the person earns.

**2. Extended Period of Eligibility (EPE).** The EPE starts the month after the 9th TWP month. During the first 36 months:
- Month at or under SGA: SSDI is paid.
- Month over SGA: SSDI is not paid (after the grace period).
- No new application is needed to restart. The person reports when earnings drop.

**3. Cessation and grace period.** The first month of SGA-level work after the TWP is the cessation month. SSDI is still paid for that month and the next two.

**4. After the 36 months.** The first month of SGA work after the 36-month re-entitlement period ends entitlement. Then the person needs Expedited Reinstatement or a new application.

**5. How SGA is measured.** SSA starts from gross earnings. It then subtracts:
- **IRWE**: impairment-related costs the worker pays.
- **Subsidies and special conditions**: support from the employer or a job coach worth more than the work's value. Examples include extra supervision, a job coach, or lower productivity at full pay. Supported employment via ERI or DVR can create a subsidy.
- **Unincurred business expenses** (self-employment).
- **Unsuccessful work attempts**: work that ended within 6 months because of the impairment is not counted (during the EPE, up to the cessation month).

The TWP test uses gross earnings. IRWE and subsidies do not lower TWP counting.

**6. Expedited Reinstatement (EXR).** If benefits stopped because of work, the person can ask to restart within 5 years. They must be unable to do SGA because of the same or a related impairment. SSA can pay up to 6 months of provisional benefits during the medical review. After 24 payable months in the Initial Reinstatement Period, the person gets a new TWP, a new EPE and new extended Medicare.

**7. Medicare while working.** Premium-free Part A continues for at least 93 months after the last TWP month, as long as the disability continues. After that, a person under 65 can buy Medicare (Part A $565/month in 2026, or $311 with 30+ quarters). Wisconsin Medicaid programs such as MAPP or a Medicare Savings Program may help **(verify)** which one fits.

**8. Overpayments.** Work causes overpayments when SSA learns about SGA months late. For new Title II overpayments, SSA withholds **50%** of the monthly benefit by default if the person does not repay within 30 days. SSA set the default to 100% for overpayments after 2025-03-27. Its current overpayment page states 50%. **(verify)** the exact date the 50% rate started, in SSA instruction EM-25029. A person can ask for a lower rate, a waiver, or an appeal. Asking within 30 days pauses collection.

## Caveats and gotchas
- SSDI has no "$1 for $2" rule. During the EPE, benefits are all or nothing by month. One dollar over SGA can mean $0 SSDI that month. An estimator must show this cliff.
- TWP months are counted even when the person did not report them. Old, unreported work often uses up TWP months without the person knowing.
- SSA can count earnings in the month they were earned, not paid. Check timing rules with WIPA.
- Childhood disability beneficiaries (CDB/DAC) and widow(er)s follow the same SGA rules, but other entitlement rules apply. **(verify)** with WIPA for these cases.
- The EXR "provisional benefits" rule (up to 6 months) comes from the Red Book SSDI-and-SSI page. The SSDI-only page does not mention it.
- 2027 Medicare premiums were not published as of 2026-10-23. SSA said 2027 premium amounts will be in my Social Security in late November 2026.

## How ERI / a hackathon tool should use this
- A work timeline tool should track TWP months, EPE months, the cessation month and the grace period. Inputs should be monthly gross earnings and month-specific SGA/TWP values.
- Ask about IRWE and subsidies (job coach, ERI supported employment). These change SGA results and are a big reason to get WIPA help.
- Always show "Estimate only. SSA makes the decision." and route the user to WIPA **1-877-826-1752**.
- Show the cliff clearly: "Earning over $1,690 in a month after your Trial Work Period may stop that month's SSDI check."
- Do NOT tell anyone a TWP month "doesn't count" or that they "won't be overpaid." Do NOT compute final overpayment amounts. Route those to WIPA, SSA, or Disability Rights Wisconsin.
