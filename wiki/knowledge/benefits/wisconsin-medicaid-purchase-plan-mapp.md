# Wisconsin Medicaid Purchase Plan (MAPP)

## TL;DR
- MAPP lets Wisconsin adults (18+) with a disability buy into full Medicaid while working. Family income can go up to **250% FPL**, and countable assets can go up to **$15,000**.
- Members must have **work activity at least once a month**. Job seekers can meet this through **Health and Employment Counseling (HEC)**, for up to 9 months plus a 3-month extension.
- There is no premium if the person's own gross monthly income is at or under 100% FPL. Above that, premiums **start at $25/month** plus 3% of net income above the FPL.
- Changes: Independence Accounts can take up to **100% of gross earnings** (was 50%), under 2025 Wisconsin Act 225, for MAPP renewals received on or after 2026-08-01 (DHS Ops Memo 26-26). The rule giving one extra month to pay before disenrollment started with December 2025 premiums. MAPP is **not** subject to the 2027 federal Medicaid work requirement.

## How it works
**Who qualifies.** Wisconsin residents age 18+, determined disabled, with family income at or under 250% FPL and individual countable assets at or under $15,000. For eligibility, the earned income deduction works like this: subtract $65, divide the rest by two, and add $65 back. IRWE and large out-of-pocket medical costs (over $500/month) are also deducted.

**Work requirement.** Members must have paid work, self-employment, or in-kind work in at least one month at a time. Volunteering does not count. There is no minimum hours or earnings amount on the DHS page. A temporary health-related exemption is available (form F-10127). Premiums are still owed during an exemption.

**Health and Employment Counseling (HEC).** HEC is for members who are not working but want to. They make an action plan and keep MAPP for up to 9 months, with a possible 3-month extension. They can use HEC twice in 5 years, with at least 6 months between uses. Application form F-00004.

**Premiums.** Premiums are based on the member's **own** income, not household income. The DHS example: $2,000 gross minus $300 allowed expenses = $1,700. Minus the 2025 FPL of $1,304.17 = $395.83. Times 3% = $11.87. Plus $25 = $36 (rounded down). Payment options include ACCESS AutoPay (drawn on the 3rd), EFT, wage withholding and check. Hardship waivers can last up to 12 months and can be backdated 3 months.

**Independence Accounts.** Members can deposit earnings into a registered Independence Account (form F-10121). It does not count toward the $15,000 asset limit. The yearly cap is 100% of gross earnings for renewals received on or after 2026-08-01 (Ops Memo 26-26). The same memo describes a $40,000 inheritance asset exclusion that takes effect later, under a future memo **(verify)**. Deposits over the cap add a penalty to the premium over the next 12 months.

**Why it matters for working people.** MAPP is the main "next step" when someone loses 1619(b) or does not qualify for SSI. Examples: an SSDI beneficiary earning above SGA, or a worker whose earnings pass the 1619(b) threshold. MAPP gives full ForwardHealth Medicaid benefits and can cover Family Care or IRIS eligibility for working members **(verify)** cost-share interaction with the ADRC.

## Caveats and gotchas
- The DHS page does not list a 2026 premium example. The 3% formula comes from P-00181 (11/2025), which used the 2025 FPL. Use the current FPL, which is $1,330/month for one person from 2026-02-01 (per DHS SSI-related and BadgerCare FPL pages).
- 250% FPL for a household of one is about $3,325/month in 2026 (our arithmetic from $1,330). **(verify)** against DHS's MAPP income table before showing it in a tool.
- How unearned income such as SSDI is treated for the premium (versus earned income) is not spelled out on the pages we opened. **(verify)** in the Medicaid Eligibility Handbook, chapter 26.
- The $40,000 inheritance exclusion is enacted (2025 Wisconsin Act 225) but not yet in effect. DHS will announce the effective date in a later memo.
- MAPP members are listed by DHS as **not subject** to the federal Medicaid work requirement that starts 2027-01-01. MAPP's own work rule still applies.
- Missing a premium no longer ends coverage right away. Coverage ends at the end of the month after the due month (first applied to December 2025 premiums, ending January 31, 2026).

## How ERI / a hackathon tool should use this
- A screener can flag "possible MAPP" when someone is working (or open to HEC), is disabled, has family income likely at or under 250% FPL, and has assets at or under $15,000. Show "Estimate only" and route the user to WIPA **1-877-826-1752** or the local income maintenance agency.
- A premium estimator must use the current-year FPL and the individual's income, and show the $25 minimum. Do not present it as a bill.
- Promote Independence Accounts as a savings tool for MAPP members.
- Do NOT say "MAPP requires X hours." No hours minimum appears in DHS materials.
- Do NOT confuse MAPP's work rule with the federal BadgerCare work requirement.
