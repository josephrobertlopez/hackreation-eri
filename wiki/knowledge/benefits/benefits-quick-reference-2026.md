# Benefits quick reference 2026

## TL;DR
- Every number below was read from a primary source on 2026-10-23. Use the 2026 column for months through December 2026.
- SSA announced 2027 values on **2026-10-14** (3.6% COLA). They take effect for January 2027 benefits. SSI for January 2027 is paid 2026-12-31.
- Rows marked "our arithmetic" are derived and should be labeled that way in tools.

## How it works
- SSA indexes the FBR, SGA, TWP, SEIE and quarter of coverage every January. The $20 and $65 exclusions, the $2,000/$3,000 resource limits and the ABLE $100,000 exclusion are fixed in law and are not indexed.
- Wisconsin amounts (supplement, FPL tables, MAPP, Family Care limits) change on Wisconsin's own schedule: May 2026 for the supplement, February for the FPL tables, January for waiver limits.
- Ticket payments use the rate in effect in the month the milestone is reached.

## Caveats and gotchas
- Row 6 is our arithmetic and ignores the Wisconsin supplement, unearned income and IRWE.
- Row 17 (1619(b)) uses the pre-May-2026 Wisconsin supplement **(verify)** at the 2027 POMS update.
- Row 27 has conflicting sources **(verify)**.
- Rows 14, 15, 17 and 20-24 for 2027 were not published as of 2026-10-23.
