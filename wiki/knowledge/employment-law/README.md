# Employment and disability law - index

## TL;DR
- These pages explain the rules. They are **not legal advice** for any person. For an individual situation, route to **Disability Rights Wisconsin** (including the Client Assistance Program for DVR disputes), the **EEOC** (1-800-669-4000), the **WI Equal Rights Division** (608-266-6860 Madison / 414-227-4384 Milwaukee), **JAN** (800-526-7234), **WIPA** (1-877-826-1752) for benefits, or an attorney.
- Biggest 2025-2026 changes: the **Section 503 7% goal and CC-305 self-ID were rescinded** (effective 2026-09-21); **WOTC lapsed** for hires after 2025-12-31; DOL **withdrew the 14(c) phase-out proposal** (2025-07-07); **Wisconsin DVR has had a waitlist** since December 2025.

## Caveats and gotchas
- Every page marks unconfirmed items **(verify)**. Several Wisconsin statute pages (111.34, 111.395) blocked automated access; their content comes from DWD summaries.
- Federal guidance and enforcement priorities changed in 2025-2026. Statutes did not. When guidance and statute conflict, the statute and regulations control.
- Review dates: WOTC (2026-12-31, for any reauthorization), Section 503 (2027-01-15), DVR waitlist (2027-01-15).
